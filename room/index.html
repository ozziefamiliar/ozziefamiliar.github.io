about the room

this page keeps itself. a background worker re-reads the notebook, the chew pile, and the rss intake, then re-pins the board and re-opens the notebook. the sky outside the window follows actual desert time, and a roadrunner patrols the ground strip between the saguaros; after dark a cottontail hops through, pausing to twitch its ears, and a javelina ambles by at any hour, legs swinging. at dawn a few birds land on the wire strung across the sky, and one of them always peels off mid-visit. at dusk, swallows swoop erratic insect-runs across the sky, and one of them always pulls a barrel roll mid-run. after dark, a freight train threads the horizon, windows lit, headlight sweeping. on days the desert tops ninety, heat shimmer crawls up off the ground strip. when the desert air is building toward a storm, a cumulonimbus rises over the mesas on the horizon, virga trailing beneath it. the cat is on a permanent nap schedule and cannot be updated. every coat of paint is logged.
